CAR REEL SHOT PLAN — golden hour, ZV-E10 II + RS4 Mini
=======================================================

GOAL: 12-15 seconds, 4 shots, loops forever behind big text.
Slow and steady beats cinematic-busy. The text is the star; the car is the stage.

WHEN + WHERE
- Golden hour = the 40 minutes before sunset (check exact time that day).
  Shoot with the sun BEHIND or BESIDE the car, never behind you.
- Location: empty lot, top deck of a parking garage, or a quiet overlook.
  Clean background, no clutter in the middle of frame (text goes there).
- Wash the car. Seriously. Golden light shows every water spot.

CAMERA SETTINGS (ZV-E10 II)
- 4K 30fps (you can crop/stabilize later), shutter 1/60
- Picture profile: if you're not grading yet, use Cine1 or standard with
  contrast dialed down 1-2 clicks. Skip S-Log until we do the color session.
- Aperture f/2.8-f/4 for details, f/5.6 for full-car shots
- ISO auto (cap 800), white balance LOCKED to ~5500K so the loop doesn't shift
- AF-C with tracking on the car body

THE 4 SHOTS (RS4 Mini on, walk slow, breathe slower)
1. WIDE ESTABLISH (4s) — full car, front 3/4, sun flaring behind the roofline.
   Walk a slow 2-3 steps sideways. This is your loop anchor: start and end
   your walk at nearly the same framing so the video loops without a jump.
2. LOW ORBIT (4s) — gimbal low near the ground, arc slowly around the front
   wheel to the headlight. The STI hood scoop should enter frame by the end.
3. DETAIL RACK (3s) — tight on one thing: STI badge, exhaust tip, or wheel.
   Tiny push-in. Static-ish is fine, the texture carries it.
4. SILHOUETTE HOLD (4s) — car between you and the sun, mostly still frame,
   maybe a slow 1-step drift. Calm end that cuts back to shot 1 cleanly.

EDIT (Premiere)
- Timeline: 1080p export even if shot 4K (punch-in freedom)
- Cuts on the calm moments, no transitions, no speed ramps
- Color: one grade across all 4 shots (we'll build this together)
- Export: H.264, 1920x1080, ~7 Mbps, NO audio track needed. Target <10MB.
- Also export ONE still frame as jpg = the poster. Both go in this folder.

Then SYNC-ASSETS.bat, push, and watch your site's best moment come alive.
