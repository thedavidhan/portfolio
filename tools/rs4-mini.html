// TOOLS · 01 · RS4 MINI SETTINGS GUIDE

Pick the shot. Get the settings.

Every setting below is what I actually run. Pick what you're shooting and it hands you the gimbal mode, camera settings, movement technique, and the post note in one screen.

RIG: DJI RS4 MINI + SONY ZV-E10 II · NO IBIS ON THIS BODY, THE GIMBAL AND THE EDIT DO THE WORK

// SETUP · DO THIS ONCE PER SHOOT

  1. Balance before power. Camera on, lens on, screen out, then balance tilt fore-aft, tilt vertical, roll, pan. It should hold any pose with motors off. A lazy balance means noisy motors and drift all day.
  2. Auto-tune. Power on, hang the rig, run auto-tune from the gimbal screen. Redo it any time you change the lens or add weight.
  3. Pair over Bluetooth, not the cable. The ZV-E10 II has one USB-C port. Pair the RS4 Mini through the camera's Bluetooth remote menu and the record button on the grip works wirelessly, leaving USB-C free for power or a monitor.
  4. Lock white balance. Auto WB drifts mid-move and ruins loops. Set it to the light you're in (about 5500K in daylight and golden hour) and leave it.
  5. Format check. 4K 30 for anything that loops or gets punched into a Reel. Crop the vertical out of the 4K in Premiere. Only switch the plate to native vertical when the subject is a person up close.

// WHEN IT FIGHTS YOU

Motors buzz / vibrateBalance is off or auto-tune is stale. Rebalance, re-run auto-tune. Usually it's the tilt axis after swapping the lens.
Horizon slowly tiltsRun horizon calibration in the gimbal settings. Park it on a flat surface first.
Footsteps in the footageThe gimbal kills rotation shake, not bounce. Heel-to-toe steps, knees bent, slower follow speed. Finish it with Warp Stabilizer in post.
Warp Stabilizer makes it jellySwitch Method from Subspace Warp to Position, Scale, Rotation. Keep Smoothness 30 to 60% for slow moves.
AF hunts mid-moveAF-C with tracking, tap the subject before you start the move. If it still hunts, widen the focus area or stop down to f/5.6 so the depth covers the miss.
Too bright for 1/60Daylight at 1/60 and f/4 will clip. Put a variable ND on the lens. No ND on you? Raise shutter to 1/125 and accept slightly crisper motion.
